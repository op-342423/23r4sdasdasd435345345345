Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: July 2026

We want you to be happy with what you ordered. If something isn't right, here's how returns, exchanges, and refunds work at THORN.

Return window

You may request a return or exchange within 14 days of your order being marked Delivered. Requests made after this window will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

Condition of returned items

  • Unworn, unwashed, and in its original condition.
  • All original tags, dust bags, and packaging included.
  • Accompanied by proof of purchase (your order number or receipt).

For hygiene reasons, some items — such as pierced jewelry that has been worn — may not be eligible for return once opened. Any such exceptions will be clearly marked on the product page.

How to start a return

Contact us with your order number and the reason for the return, using the phone number or social links on our homepage. We'll confirm your eligibility and let you know the next steps, including where to send the item if a physical return is needed.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we'll notify you of the outcome. Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days, using the same payment method you used to pay — or as store credit, if you'd prefer and paid by Cash on Delivery.

Exchanges

If you'd like a different size or item instead of a refund, let us know when you request your return — we'll arrange the exchange once the original item is received in eligible condition, subject to stock availability.

Damaged or defective items

If your item arrives damaged or with a manufacturing fault, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the issue. We'll cover the cost of return shipping and send a replacement or full refund — this is on us, not a standard return.

Return shipping costs

Unless the item is damaged, defective, or not as described, return shipping costs for a change-of-mind return are the customer's responsibility.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, reach out before sending anything back — we're glad to help sort it out.
