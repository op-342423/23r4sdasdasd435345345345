Announcement bar

A slim bar above the nav on every page — good for sale announcements, shipping notes, or drop reminders. Shoppers can dismiss it, but it comes back for a new message if you change the text later.

Coupons

Create a discount and choose how customers get it: type Auto-apply on and it's applied for everyone (or a specific list of emails) with no code needed, or leave it off and share the code yourself — either way you can also show it as a banner across the top of the site. Set a start/expiry date and it simply stops working on its own once that window passes (nothing is deleted — you can still see it here, re-enable it, or remove it for good with Delete).

Payment methods

Turn payment methods on or off and set the order customers see them in at checkout — that order is exactly what renders on the checkout page. Cash on Delivery works out of the box; Vodafone Cash and InstaPay need your phone/username (and optionally a QR code) filled in below before you enable them. Visa and Mastercard are placeholders only for now — customers will see them as "coming soon" until Stripe is connected later.

Learn More section

A full-screen cinematic section that appears below the hero. Clicking "Learn more" on the shop page smooth-scrolls down to it instead of opening a popup. Everything below is optional except the title — leave the section disabled until you're happy with it.

Collection stories

Each collection gets its own story block on the homepage — title, mood, a short story, and a large photo or video — instead of just being a category filter. Add as many as you like; they appear in the order below, alternating left/right automatically.

Editorial

Pure storytelling for the homepage grid below the shop — campaign photography, runway or behind-the-scenes clips, and quotes. Never shows a price. Mark an item “Large” to make it span two grid cells.
