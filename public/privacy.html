Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains what information THORN collects when you browse or buy from us, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this site, you agree to the practices described below.

Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly, such as your name, email address, phone number, and delivery address when you create an account, place an order, or write a review. If you pay by Vodafone Cash or InstaPay, you may optionally share a payment reference or screenshot with your order to help us confirm it faster.

We also collect information automatically: the items in your cart (stored in your browser, not on our servers, until checkout), pages you visit, and basic technical details like browser type, so the site works correctly and loads quickly.

How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders, and to keep you updated on their status.
  • To maintain your account, wishlist, and order history.
  • To respond to questions, reviews, and customer service requests.
  • To improve the site, our catalog, and how we communicate with customers.
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse of the site.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information. We share only what's necessary to fulfill your order — for example, your name, phone number, and address with the courier who delivers it. If you pay by card in the future, payment details are handled directly by our payment processor and are never stored on our servers.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your cart between visits and to keep you signed in. We don't use third-party advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

Data retention

We keep account and order information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to meet legal, accounting, or dispute-resolution requirements. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time using the contact details below.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us using the details on the homepage or in the footer of this site, and we'll respond as soon as we can.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the shop grows. Significant changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Reach out through the phone number or social links on our homepage.
