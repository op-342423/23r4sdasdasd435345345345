Shipping Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Here's what to expect once you place an order with THORN — from processing to your doorstep.

Processing time

Orders are reviewed and prepared within 1–2 business days of being placed. You'll see the status change from Pending to Accepted in "Your orders" once we've confirmed it.

Delivery time

Once accepted, most orders arrive within an estimated 2–4 days, depending on your location. You can follow your order's progress at any time — Pending → Accepted → Out for delivery → Delivered — from the Your orders page.

Delivery fee

A flat delivery fee is added at checkout and shown clearly before you confirm your order — there are no hidden charges added later.

Payment on delivery

Cash on Delivery is available on every order: you pay the courier when your package arrives. If Vodafone Cash or InstaPay is enabled at checkout, you can pay ahead of time and optionally attach a payment reference or screenshot to help us confirm it quickly.

Address accuracy

Please double-check your delivery address and phone number before confirming your order. If our courier can't reach you at the details provided, delivery may be delayed, and re-delivery attempts may take longer than the original estimate.

Delays, damage, or missing items

If your order arrives damaged, incomplete, or hasn't arrived within a reasonable time past the estimate, contact us right away using the details on our homepage. We'll investigate with the courier and make it right — see our Refund & Return Policy for how that works.

Order tracking

If you created an account at checkout, every order and its current status is available any time from Your orders.
